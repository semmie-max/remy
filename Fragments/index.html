I’m made up of a few contradictions. If I had to describe myself, I’d probably need a few attempts.
I’m somewhat lost between the immensity of space and the elegance of engineering, the depth of the sea and the inexplicable language of music.
I have an affection for playful, minimalist things, and an unreasonable curiosity for whatever lies beyond what I already understand.
I make, I wander, I wonder. I’m still trying to decide whether I’m searching for answers or simply collecting better questions.
And somewhere along the way, I intend to find enough great music to last me a lifetime.
